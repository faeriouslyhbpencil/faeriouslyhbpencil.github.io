Being abroad and my current revelations:

I've been abroad for almost two months now, completing a semester of my university studies. It's been an eye-opening experience, not only because I've gotten the change to immerse myself in a different culture; but also one that has been dormant in my roots and a direct connection back to my heritage. I've explored a new part of the world and even more than that, I 've discovered a part of myself that has been lying dormant for a large part of my life. This journey was mean't to test how well I do on my own and push me out of my comfort zone. I initially found myself struggling to speak, not because I didn't know the language, but because I was nervous. I gained my confidence fast enough and have made many connections here which I am very greatful for. My self-reliancy skills were put to the test and for the most part, I feel like I have grown immensely as a person, though it hasn't been all that long. I'm looking forward to more experiences and I hope to make life-long friendships in the process.

xoxo, Hasna
