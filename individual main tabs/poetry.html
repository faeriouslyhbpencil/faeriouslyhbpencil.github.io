Entry 02

Colour

2023/31/03

When I look at you, the entire world fades. You are the only visible colour in my monochrome world. One single glance at you and the shielded walls around my heart drop as if they were never there. I wish I could ask you to stay by my side. I wish I could ask you to be mine. The words melt on my tongue before I even get the chance to utter them. The world continues to spin, You still seem unfazed. I hope one day you'll come to realize my feelings for you will forever stay the same, but for now, with every passing moment I shall remain your Juliette Awaiting for you to take your rightful place
